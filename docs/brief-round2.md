# Round 2: a live change to your own service

Round 2 is a **90-minute** video session, held only if your round 1 submission passes. You work on **your own round 1 repository**, sharing your screen. **No AI assistants** during the session: no chat assistant, no code completion beyond what your editor does without AI.

## How it runs

1. **About 10 minutes**: you walk us through your solution and the choices in your `REPORT.md`.
2. **About 70 minutes**: the interviewer picks one change request for your service and you implement it. It touches the parts you built in round 1 (search, reservations or the assistant). You decide how to approach it, what to test, and what to leave out; think aloud as you go.
3. **About 10 minutes**: we discuss what you did, what you would do with more time, and how you would know it works in production.

## Before the session

- Have your repository cloned, building and running on the machine you will use. A build from scratch during the session costs you time.
- Have your usual tools ready: editor, terminal, database client, HTTP client.
- You may read documentation and search the web, as long as no AI tool answers for you.

## What we look at

- Whether you understand your own code well enough to change it under time pressure.
- How you break down an unfamiliar request, and the questions you ask about it.
- How you check that the change works and that you did not break the rest, the four round 1 conditions included.
- The trade-offs you name, and the ones you make on purpose.

Finishing the change is not required. A clear partial change with a clear account of what is left beats a rushed complete one.
